# Support Dashboard Redesign Interview
*Interview · 00:10:00 · Source: interview.srt*

## TL;DR
Rhea interviewed Dev about redesigning a support dashboard. Dev described combining tickets and account history into one tested view, and the lessons learned about realistic data and involving support staff early.

## Summary
Rhea asked Dev about a product project that taught them something. Dev described redesigning a support dashboard after interviews showed staff lost time switching between tickets and account history. The team prioritised one combined view and used prototype sessions to test whether staff could find the last contact without opening another page. Dev learned that an attractive screen was not enough and that realistic ticket histories were needed to reveal missing context. Disagreements about how much information belonged on the main screen were settled by testing alternatives rather than arguing preference. Dev would involve support staff earlier next time because their terminology differed from internal labels. Dev led the interviews, built the prototype and documented findings, while engineers owned implementation.

## Key Points
- Staff lost time switching between tickets and account history, so the team built one combined view [01:00]
