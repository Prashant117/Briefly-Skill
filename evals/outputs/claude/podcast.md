# Focus, Interruptions and Meeting Habits
*Podcast · 00:08:00 · Source: podcast.srt*

## TL;DR
On Workbench, host Leena and guest Omar discussed focus and meeting habits. Leena gave listeners two recommendations, and the next episode will cover asynchronous status updates.

## Summary
Leena introduced guest Omar, who studies how teams share information, for a discussion of focus and meeting habits. Omar argued that frequent context switching makes careful work harder, presenting this as his interpretation of teams he has observed rather than a measured universal figure. He added that notifications are only one source of interruption, since unclear priorities can force task switching even when devices are quiet. Omar said a useful agenda tells people what kind of contribution is needed, because discussions and decisions need different preparation, and that no single meeting duration works for everyone. He noted written context can help colleagues across time zones if they can find it and ask questions. Leena recommended a distraction-free work block and reading agendas before calls.
